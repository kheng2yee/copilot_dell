# Glossary — Project Phoenix

- **Phoenix** — Codename for the customer portal redesign project.
- **Compass** — Separate, related project covering the native mobile app (out of scope for Phoenix).
- **GA** — General Availability; the full public launch, targeted for 30 November 2026.
- **Alpha** — Internal-only test build (August 2026).
- **Beta** — Limited rollout to 10% of customers (October 2026).
- **RACI** — Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed — the decision-ownership framework used on this project.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA** — Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the accessibility standard Phoenix must meet.
- **Portal** — Shorthand for the customer-facing account management website being redesigned.
- **SSO** — Single sign-on; part of the account migration scope in Phoenix.
- **PDPA** — Personal Data Protection Act 2010, Malaysia's law governing how customer personal data is collected, stored, and processed. All Phoenix features touching customer PII need Legal & Compliance sign-off.
- **Meridian Rewards** — Meridian Retail's customer loyalty points programme, being integrated into the new dashboard.
- **DuitNow / FPX** — Local Malaysian payment rails (real-time transfer and online banking respectively) that the portal must display as payment options.
- **BM / EN** — Bahasa Malaysia / English — the two languages the portal must support.
- **MYT** — Malaysia Time (UTC+8); the time zone used for all project scheduling.
- **Klang Valley** — The greater Kuala Lumpur metropolitan region; Meridian's largest customer base.
- **Sprint board** — The Planner board used to track weekly engineering and design tasks.
- **Steering review** — Monthly meeting with the Executive Sponsor to review progress and risks.
