## FAQ

**Q: What's the current phase of the project?**
A: As of the last status update, the team is in the design-system-finalization phase, targeting completion by 1 June 2026.

**Q: Where do I find the design files?**
A: Figma, workspace "Phoenix Portal." Request access from Aiman Haziq.

**Q: Is there a native mobile app in this project?**
A: No — that's a separate initiative called "Project Compass." Phoenix covers the web portal only, with a mobile-responsive layout (important given ~85% of our traffic is mobile).

**Q: Who approves scope changes?**
A: Dana Whitfield (Executive Sponsor), routed through Priya Menon (PM).

**Q: What's the meeting cadence?**
A: Daily standup (async in Teams), weekly Phoenix sync on Thursdays at 10:00 AM MYT, and a monthly steering review with Dana Whitfield.

**Q: Where do I report a bug?**
A: File it in the GitHub repo under Issues, tag Leo Tran (QA Lead) for triage.

**Q: Does the portal need to support Bahasa Malaysia?**
A: Yes — the portal ships in both Bahasa Malaysia and English. Nurul Izzati owns copy for both languages.

**Q: What do I need to know about PDPA before touching customer data?**
A: All new team members must complete the PDPA awareness module in their first week. Any feature touching customer PII needs Legal & Compliance review before build — check with Priya Menon.

**Q: Are there local payment methods to account for?**
A: Yes — the portal displays DuitNow and FPX alongside card payments; this is part of Phoenix's scope.
