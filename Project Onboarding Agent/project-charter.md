# Project Charter — Project Phoenix

**Project name:** Project Phoenix (Customer Portal Redesign)
**Company:** Meridian Retail Sdn Bhd (HQ: Bangsar South, Kuala Lumpur)
**Sponsor:** Dana Whitfield, VP of Customer Experience
**Project Manager:** Priya Menon
**Start date:** 2 March 2026
**Target launch:** 30 November 2026
**Time zone for all project times:** MYT (UTC+8)

## Background
Meridian Retail Sdn Bhd's current customer portal (last redesigned in 2019) has a bounce rate of 42% on the account management pages and generates the highest volume of support tickets of any digital property, across our Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru customer base. Project Phoenix will replace it with a modern, mobile-first portal.

## Goals
1. Reduce account-management-related support tickets by 30% within 3 months of launch.
2. Improve mobile task-completion rate from 54% to 80% (mobile-first, given ~85% of Meridian's traffic is on mobile).
3. Consolidate order tracking, returns, loyalty points (Meridian Rewards), and account settings into a single unified dashboard.
4. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.
5. Ensure full compliance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) for all customer data handling.

## Scope
**In scope:** Web portal redesign, mobile-responsive layout, new account dashboard, returns flow, Meridian Rewards loyalty integration, single sign-on migration, DuitNow/FPX payment method display, support for Bahasa Malaysia and English.
**Out of scope:** Native mobile app (separate project — "Project Compass"), backend order-management system changes, marketing site redesign.

## Key milestones
| Milestone | Target date |
|---|---|
| Discovery & research complete | 15 April 2026 |
| Design system finalized | 1 June 2026 |
| Alpha build (internal testing) | 15 August 2026 |
| Beta (10% customer rollout) | 1 October 2026 |
| General availability | 30 November 2026 |

Note: milestone dates are set with buffer around Hari Raya Aidilfitri (approx. late March 2026) and Merdeka Day (31 August) to account for reduced team availability.

## Budget
Approved budget: **RM 5,200,000**, covering design, engineering, QA contractors, and a 3-month post-launch support buffer.

## Compliance & data residency
Customer data must remain hosted within Malaysia-based data centers (or approved APAC region with data processing agreements in place) per PDPA requirements. Legal review required before any new third-party integration that touches customer PII.

## Success criteria
Project is considered successful if GA ships by end of Q4 2026 and support ticket volume drops by 30% within 90 days post-launch.
